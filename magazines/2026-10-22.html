Macro Overview

Macro

Treasury yields remain elevated with the 10-year at 5.12% and the 2-year at 4.79%, maintaining a positively sloped curve of +33 basis points — a normalised posture following the prolonged inversion of 2023–2024. Fixed Income

Fixed Income

The 10-year Treasury yield stands at 5.12%, while the 2-year yields 4.79%, producing a positive term spread of +33 basis points. The curve has normalised from its prolonged inversion, now steepening modestly as longer-dated yields are pushed higher by persistent growth and inflation expectations. At these levels, the real rate environment remains a significant headwind for growth equities and duration-sensitive fixed income instruments. The 5.25% area in the 10-year is the key technical level for bond market participants to watch.

Currency Markets

Currencies

EUR/USD trades at 1.1246, holding in the upper portion of its recent range as dollar strength is tempered by improved euro-area sentiment and narrowing rate differentials. Commodities

Commodities

Gold spot trades at $4,099.69 per troy ounce, consolidating near all-time highs and reflecting persistent central bank demand, geopolitical hedging, and structural de-dollarisation flows. The $4,100 threshold is acting as near-term resistance. WTI crude eased 1.93% on the session to $107.89 per barrel, giving back some of its sharp recent gains after touching $110 in the prior session. Nevertheless, crude remains more than 13% above its levels of just two weeks ago (~$95), driven by OPEC+ supply discipline and elevated geopolitical risk premiums. Sustained oil above $100 rekindles stagflation concerns and adds complexity to the Fed's policy outlook.

The U.S. Treasury curve sits in mildly positive territory: the 10-year yield at 5.12% and the 2-year at 4.83% produce a +29 basis point spread. The absence of a fresh inversion signal is modestly constructive for the economic outlook, but the absolute rate environment remains a headwind.

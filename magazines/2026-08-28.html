Volatility

The CBOE VIX settled at 14.51, declining 4.82% on the session and firmly in "complacent" territory (below 15). Markets are pricing very limited near-term tail risk despite a packed earnings calendar — DELL, AVGO, SNOW, and MongoDB all report next week — and elevated single-stock implied volatility in AI names ahead of NVDA. The divergence between low macro vol and high single-stock vol is a structural feature of the AI-era equity market, where idiosyncratic earnings catalysts dominate index-level calm.

Section 5

Fixed Income

The 10-year Treasury yield stands at 4.66% and the 2-year at 4.19%, producing a curve spread of +47 basis points — a positively sloped, modestly steepening configuration. The curve's normalization from its prior inversion reflects expectations of a gradual Fed easing cycle ahead, though the persistence of elevated short-end yields confirms that the market is not pricing aggressive cuts in the near term. Term premium demand at the long end is being supported by fiscal deficit concerns and robust auction demand from international buyers, keeping the curve's steepening orderly rather than disruptive.

Section 6

Currencies

EUR/USD trades at 1.1649, near multi-month highs, underpinned by a narrowing in US-EU rate differentials and resilient eurozone data that has prevented a sustained dollar breakout. USD/JPY sits at 159.49, near levels that previously prompted Japanese Ministry of Finance verbal intervention, keeping traders alert to official yen-support actions. The yen's persistent weakness reflects the structural carry trade dynamic: as long as the Fed-BoJ rate differential remains wide, yen-funded equity and carry positions will sustain dollar demand, though intervention risk limits the downside beyond 160.

Commodities

Gold trades at $4,605.82/oz, a marginal retreat from recent record highs as a modest pullback in spot prices triggered profit-taking in gold mining equities — Agnico Eagle (AEM) shed 3.56% on Thursday on combined pressure from lower gold prices and elevated capital expenditure guidance. The WTI trend is bearish short-term, though geopolitical risk floor support persists.

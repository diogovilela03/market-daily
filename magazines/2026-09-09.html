Section 05
Fixed Income

The 10-year Treasury yield stands at 4.78% and the 2-year at 4.37%, producing a 10Y–2Y spread of +41 basis points — a steepening curve. After a prolonged inversion that dominated 2022–2024, the curve's return to positive territory normally signals improving economic expectations; however, in the current context it also reflects longer-dated yields rising faster than short-dated ones as the market digests stronger-than-expected growth data. News reports flagging 30-year yields at near 19-year highs underscore the breadth of the move. Mortgage-sensitive sectors — housing, financials — remain under pressure.

Section 06
Currencies

EUR/USD at 1.1644 reflects a surprisingly resilient euro, even as the hot US jobs print reinforced dollar-bullish expectations. The pair's relative stability may reflect the ECB's own hawkish posture keeping rate differentials from widening further. USD/JPY at 153.09 sits in the zone that has historically triggered verbal warnings from Japanese officials; a move toward 155 would likely accelerate discussion of direct intervention, as the Bank of Japan faces the difficult task of normalizing policy without triggering a disorderly yen move.

Section 07
Commodities

Gold at $4,405/oz remains near historically elevated levels, driven by persistent inflation concerns, de-dollarization trends among central banks, and safe-haven demand amid rate uncertainty. Precious metals mining companies like Wheaton Precious Metals (WPM) are reporting record revenues at these prices. WTI crude at $91.48/bbl (most recent daily) reflects OPEC+ production discipline maintaining a supply floor above $90; prices at this level risk re-accelerating headline CPI, directly complicating the Fed's inflation-fighting mandate and potentially forcing the hand of additional monetary tightening.

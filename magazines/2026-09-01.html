Fixed Income

The US Treasury curve continues to steepen: the 10-year yield stands at 4.73% and the 2-year at 4.34%, producing a 39-basis-point positive spread. The curve's un-inversion — which began as the Fed signaled peak-rate clarity — is now firmly established, reflecting markets pricing long-term growth and inflation risks while the short end anchors on a still-restrictive policy rate. Higher long-end yields are proving broadly supportive for life insurers and banks with duration exposure, as detailed in recent industry analysis. No major Treasury auctions or Fed communication events are flagged imminently this week.

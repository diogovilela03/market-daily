Section 4
Fixed Income

The US 10-year Treasury yield stands at 5.24% (as of October 9), with the 2-year at 4.80% — a +44bps spread representing a modestly upward-sloping curve after months of near-flat or inverted territory. The persistence of 5%+ long-end yields tightens financial conditions meaningfully, acting as a continuous headwind for equity multiples and extending the pain for duration-sensitive sectors. Curve direction will be key: further steepening could signal markets are pricing growth re-acceleration, while a flattening move would entrench the higher-for-longer narrative into 2027.

Currencies

EUR/USD: 1.1209 — the euro holds above the key 1.12 figure, supported by the ECB's own tightening cycle, which has narrowed the rate differential advantage the dollar previously enjoyed. The pair remains in a technically constructive posture above 1.10 support, though any hawkish surprise from the Fed could reassert dollar strength into Q4. USD/JPY: 158.26 — the yen remains near multi-decade lows, well above the 155 intervention threshold that drew Bank of Japan verbal warnings earlier in the year. At these levels, the risk of coordinated FX intervention or an unexpected BOJ policy shift rises materially; options markets are pricing elevated yen volatility into year-end.

Section 6
Commodities

Gold at $4,148.74/oz continues its secular bull run, extending gains driven by central bank reserve diversification away from dollar-denominated assets, elevated geopolitical uncertainty, and real yield stabilisation at levels that reduce the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding stores of value. The $4,000 level has now established itself as a support floor. WTI crude at $96.24/bbl remains firm near the psychologically significant $100 ceiling; OPEC+ production discipline, robust demand from emerging Asian economies, and a persistent geopolitical risk premium are collectively keeping crude elevated despite headwinds from a moderating U.S. industrial cycle.

Section 4

Fixed Income

The 10-year Treasury yield is at 5.13% and the 2-year at 4.81%, yielding a 2Y–10Y spread of +32 basis points. The curve is in a modestly normal slope with a steepening bias, having reversed the prolonged inversion seen earlier in the cycle. At these nominal levels, real yields remain deeply restrictive and continue to act as a structural headwind to equity multiples, particularly in long-duration growth sectors. The slight uptick in both tenors over the past week (10Y: from 5.09% on Oct 16 to 5.13% Oct 21; 2Y: from 4.74% to 4.81%) suggests ongoing upward yield pressure.

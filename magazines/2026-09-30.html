Fixed Income

The US Treasury curve steepened modestly, with the 10-year yield at 5.24% and the 2-year at 4.92%, producing a positive spread of +32 basis points. The curve remains off its most inverted levels seen earlier in the cycle, suggesting markets are beginning to price a more normalized long-run rate environment — though at levels that still represent meaningful restraint for borrowers. The long-bond ETF TLT fell 0.50% on the session, confirming ongoing selling pressure at the long end as investors reprice duration risk.

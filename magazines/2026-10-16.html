Section 04
Fixed Income

The US Treasury curve remains positively sloped: the 10-year yield ticked down 2bp to 5.19% while the 2-year yield rose 3bp to 4.82%, leaving the 10Y–2Y spread at +37bp. The mild steepening at the long end, while the short end held firm, is consistent with markets tentatively pricing in eventual easing but refusing to collapse the term premium in the face of persistent inflation. The TLT long-bond ETF was among the most actively traded issues (+1.37%), suggesting some tactical buyers are beginning to re-engage duration at these elevated yield levels.

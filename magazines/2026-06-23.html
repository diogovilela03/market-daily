Macro

The Federal Reserve's decision under Chair Kevin Warsh to hold rates while publishing dot-plot projections hinting at a future tightening marks a decisive pivot away from the easing path markets had priced in — PVH fell 6% and multiple consumer discretionary names sold off in its wake. The 10-year Treasury yield stands at 4.46% and the 2-year at 4.19%, a modest +27 basis-point spread that reflects a mildly normal curve; the risk is that the short end climbs as rate-hike expectations solidify, potentially re-inverting the curve and tightening financial conditions. A broader geopolitical recalibration — anchored by the US-Iran peace deal and the Strait of Hormuz reopening — has reshuffled risk premiums across commodities, currencies, and emerging markets, creating a mixed macro backdrop where deflationary energy tailwinds compete with inflationary monetary signals.

Section 04

Volatility

The CBOE VIX closed at 17.28, up +0.88 points (+5.09%) on the session — comfortably below the 20-handle that marks elevated fear, but meaningfully above recent complacency troughs. The single-session spike reflects options markets beginning to reprice near-term uncertainty in response to the Fed's hawkish pivot; the VIX moving sharply higher even while equities registered relatively modest declines suggests traders are hedging against the potential for a more disorderly repricing should incoming economic data support a rate-hike narrative.

Section 05

Fixed Income

The 10-year Treasury yield sits at 4.46% and the 2-year at 4.19%, a spread of approximately +27 basis points — a modestly normal upward-sloping curve that is a far cry from the deep inversions of 2023–2024. Chair Warsh's hawkish tone risks pushing the short end higher: if the 2-year climbs back toward or above the 10-year, re-inversion would signal renewed recession concern and add another headwind for equities. Bond traders will be closely monitoring upcoming economic data — particularly inflation prints and employment figures — to determine whether the Fed's hawkish pivot is justified by the data or represents a policy overshoot.
